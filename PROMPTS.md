# AI usage log

Record genuine AI interactions as you work. Do not invent dates, tool outputs, defects, fixes, or commit links. Add an entry immediately after each meaningful AI-assisted task.

## Entry template

### YYYY-MM-DD — [tool and task]
- **Prompt / request:** What you actually asked.
- **Tool output:** What it generated.
- **What was wrong or incomplete:** The specific defect you found (or state that none was found after review).
- **What I changed:** Your actual correction and how you verified it.
- **Commit:** Link to the real commit, once created.

At least five genuine entries are required by the challenge guidance; include at least two about authorisation/access control/audit security if that matches the work you actually perform.
